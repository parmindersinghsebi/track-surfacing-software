Import survey data

Expected data: 10 m station number, optional Chainage (remark/reference only), and existing rail level (RL in metres). RL and lift calculations always follow the 10 m station sequence and the configured station spacing. Chainage is never used to calculate distance, sleeper count, RL, or lift; it is displayed only as a remark.

Important: This is a planning/calculation aid, not an approved railway design system. Do not feed its output directly to a tamping machine until the calculation method, survey datum, design profile, lift/lowering limits, obligatory points and machine data have been independently checked and approved by the competent railway authority.
